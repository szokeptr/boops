# Sidebar sections

Group your bots and group chats into sections of your own, such as **Work** or **Home**. A section can hold both bots and group chats. Anything you haven't sorted stays in the usual **Bots** and **Group chats** lists.

## Make a section

Click **New section** at the bottom of the sidebar, or right-click a bot or group chat and choose **Move to section → New section…**. On mobile, tap **New section** at the end of the list, or long-press a bot or chat.

## Sort into sections

Drag a bot or group chat onto a section, or between rows to set its place. Drop it on **Bots** or **Group chats** to take it out of its section. You can also right-click it, or long-press on mobile, and choose **Move to section**.

Drag a section's header to reorder sections. Click a header to fold it; a folded section shows how many it holds and a dot when one of them needs you. Right-click a header, or long-press on mobile, to rename, move or delete it. Deleting a section keeps its bots and chats.

Your sections follow you to every device and stay private to you. People you share bots with arrange their own. A search on mobile lists matching bots without sections.
