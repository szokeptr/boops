# Group chats

A group chat puts you, the people you invited, and your bots in one conversation. Bots stay quiet until someone mentions them, then answer in front of everyone.

## Start one

Click **+** next to **Group chats** in the sidebar, or open the command palette and choose **New group chat**. Pick the bots and people, and give it a name or leave the one Boops suggests. The first time, Boops also asks what everyone should call you; you can change it later in **Settings → Members → Group chats**.

People you invited can start group chats too, once you turn on **Can start group chats** for them in **Settings → Members**. They can bring only the bots you shared with them.

## Talk to a bot

Type `@` and pick a bot, or write its name after `@`. Only the bots you mention answer. Each one reads what was said since it last spoke, so you can talk among yourselves first and bring a bot in when you need it. A bot you add to a chat catches up on its last 20 messages.

Bots can mention each other to hand work along. After a few hand-offs they stop and wait for a person.

Attach photos and files with the paperclip, by pasting, or by dropping them on the message box. A bot you mention gets the files sent since it last spoke.

## Approvals and questions

When a bot needs a go-ahead or an answer, a card appears above the message box. The person whose message the bot is working on answers it; you can always answer it too. Everyone else sees who it is waiting on.

## What bots share

A bot answers a group chat with what it knows: its notes, its files, and the Connections you shared with members. It doesn't repeat your private conversations with it. While anyone you invited is in the chat, the bot uses only the Connections you shared with members. Work a bot does for someone runs at that person's access.

## Notifications

Every message notifies everyone else in the chat on their phones. Mute a chat from its menu, or right-click it in the sidebar, to silence it.

## Leave, archive or delete

Anyone can leave a chat. Whoever started it, and you as owner, can add or remove people and bots, rename it, archive it, or delete it. Archived chats sit under **Archived** below your group chats until you unarchive them. You can see and delete chats that people you invited started without you in **Settings → Members**.
